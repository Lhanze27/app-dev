# 🕵️‍♂️ Shoshimin: How to Become Ordinary

Welcome to my little corner of the internet dedicated to one of my favorite anime series — **Shoshimin: How to Become Ordinary**. If you're into quiet mysteries, subtle character dynamics, and stories that unfold like origami, you're in for a treat.

---

## ✨ What’s It About?

Two high school students, Jougorou Kobato and Yuki Osanai, make a pact to live as “shoshimin” — ordinary citizens who avoid drama and attention. But of course, life doesn’t play by their rules. Despite their best efforts, they keep getting pulled into strange little mysteries around school and town.

> “We just want to be ordinary.”  
> — Kobato, probably while solving a mystery he swore he wouldn’t touch

---

## 👥 Main Characters

- **Jougorou Kobato** – Smart, observant, and constantly fighting the urge to play detective.
- **Yuki Osanai** – Loves sweets, hates trouble, and somehow always ends up in the middle of it.
- **The World Around Them** – Classmates, teachers, and strangers who bring chaos to their quiet lives.

---

## 🎭 Why I Love It

- The mysteries are small but meaningful — like finding out who stole a cake or why a rumor started.
- Kobato and Osanai’s dynamic is *chef’s kiss* — awkward, clever, and full of tension.
- It’s beautifully animated, with a soft color palette that matches the tone perfectly.
- It’s from the same author as *Hyouka*, so if you liked that, this is a must-watch.
